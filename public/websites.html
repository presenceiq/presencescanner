Common questions

What does $199 actually get me?

One page, built by hand from what you tell me about your business. Your services written out specifically, your service area, your real customer reviews as text, a tap-to-call button, and a set of questions and answers customers actually ask. Plus the technical work underneath that makes it readable to Google and to AI tools: structured data, a sitemap, a robots file that welcomes the AI crawlers, and submission to both Google and Bing.

Why one page and not five?

Because one page carries the heavy work. Everything that makes you findable, your services, your reviews, your questions and answers and the technical work underneath, all sits on that page and all of it counts. Five thin pages perform worse than one good one. Start with the one, live with it a while, and add pages later if you find you actually need them. They are $99 each and they are not going anywhere.

Who owns the domain and the website?

You do, both. The domain is bought in your own name, with your own card. The hosting sits in your own account, free at this size, and you invite me in to do the work. You can remove me with two clicks whenever you like, and the files are yours on request.

Why is the hosting free? What happens if that changes?

Cloudflare's own documentation says requests to static assets are free and unlimited. It is not a trial or a promotion. They already run that network for their paying customers, so serving a small site like yours costs them nothing extra, and some free users eventually become paying ones. That is the whole model. If they ever did start charging, their paid plan starts at $5 a month, but the better answer is that it would barely matter. Your site is plain files with no database behind it, so moving it somewhere else takes minutes. You are not locked in to anybody, including me.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay the $199 once. The only ongoing cost is your domain renewal, which goes to the registrar rather than to me. They set that price and it can change, so check it when you buy.

How long does it take?

A few days, usually. The build itself takes under an hour. What takes the time is you and I talking through what goes on it, and then waiting for the domain to connect.

What if I do not like it?

You get one round of changes included. Tell me what is wrong and I fix it. Typos and wrong phone numbers are always free to fix, whenever you spot them.

I already have a Facebook page. Is that not enough?

Facebook is rented ground and it is hard for search engines and AI tools to read. When someone asks ChatGPT for a painter in North Port, it draws on web pages it can read, not on your Facebook posts. A page of your own on your own domain is something you control and something those tools can quote.

Do I have to be on the PresenceScanner directory?

No. They are separate things. The directory listing is free and always will be. This is paid work on a site of your own.

What makes this different from Wix or Squarespace?

Those give you a template and you do the work. This is written for you, by a person, from a conversation about your business. And the part most template sites skip is the part that matters now: the structured data, the visible questions and answers, and being submitted properly so Google and AI tools can find it.
