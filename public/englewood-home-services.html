What to ask before you hire anybody in Florida

Ask all five of whoever you call, including the businesses on this page and including me.

Are you licensed and insured for this work, and can I see it? Anybody who carries both will not mind being asked. Florida licenses can be checked free at myfloridalicense.com.

Who is actually doing the work? The owner, an employee or a sub. And who do you call afterward if something is wrong.

Is the estimate in writing, with what is not included? The exclusions are where the arguments come from, so get those in writing too.

When do you want paying, and how much up front? A large deposit before anybody has been to the house is worth a second thought.

Have you done this on a house like mine? A tile roof, a pool cage and a stucco wall are different jobs, and experience on one is not experience on another.
