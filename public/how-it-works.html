Common questions

If several businesses in my trade are listed, how does AI choose which to recommend?

Google and AI tools favor the business they have the most clear, consistent information about — the one with the most reviews, the most complete details, and the closest match to the customer's specific question. Detail wins. The more specific you are about exactly what you do, where you serve, and what sets you apart, the better AI can match you to the right customer. Vague, generic pages blur together.

What's the single most important thing I can do to get found?

Google reviews, by far. A steady flow of real, recent reviews is a top factor in how Google ranks a business and how AI tools decide which to recommend. A business with many fresh reviews gets recommended over a similar business with few. Gather them honestly — ask every customer, never gate or incentivize — to stay within platform rules.

Is a listing really free?

Yes — a professional page, no cost, no ads, no catch. Pages are hand-built for local businesses in the community, to help neighbors find trusted local services and help good businesses get found online.
