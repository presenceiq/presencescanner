What "getting found" actually includes

When someone takes this on, whether that's you or me, here's the real work involved:

  • Google Business Profile setup and cleanup, the right categories, your accurate service area, hours, photos, and a full list of services.
  • Consistency across the web, making sure your name, phone, and address match everywhere your business appears, so Google and AI trust the information.
  • Review strategy, getting the steady flow of real customer reviews that drives ranking.
  • A website that answers the right questions, so both Google and AI can understand and recommend you.
  • AI-search readiness, making sure the pieces are in place for ChatGPT and other AI tools to recognize your business.

It's not complicated. But it's detailed, and it takes time to do right.
