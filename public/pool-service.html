A pool is more than the pool guy

Most pool problems in Florida aren't actually the water. The cage rusts, the deck stains, the pump trips a breaker, the salt cell dies, the heater won't light. Those are five different trades, and calling a pool service for any of them wastes everybody's afternoon. Here's who on the directory does which part.

What to ask before you hire a pool service in Florida

These are the five that separate quotes you can compare from quotes you can't. Ask all of them, including of the two companies on this page.

Do you repair equipment, or only clean? This is the big one, and it's why there are two different companies above. A cleaning route and a repair business are not the same thing, and finding out in August when the heater dies is the wrong time.

Is the price weekly or monthly, and are chemicals included? Two quotes can look close together and land a long way apart once chemicals are in on one and out on the other. Get it in writing either way.

What happens when the pool goes green after a storm? Ask whether a recovery is covered by the regular rate or billed separately, and what it typically runs. Afterward is too late to ask.

Who actually shows up, and is it the same person? A rotating tech doesn't know what your water did last month. Ask how many pools a day their route carries.

Do you hold a Florida pool or spa contractor license, and what's the number? Ask for it, then check it free at myfloridalicense.com. PresenceScanner doesn't verify anybody's license, so this one is on you.
