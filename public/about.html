Why I built this directory

I'm not a big company. I'm a local small business owner, just like you — and I paid for this myself, because I believe we all do better when we're easier to find.

Who I am

My name is Michael Putnam. I live in Nokomis and I run three local businesses right here in Southwest Florida — a handyman business, my wife's house cleaning business, and a real estate business. So I know exactly what it's like to try to get found, to fight with Google, to wonder why the phone isn't ringing.

I've spent 15 years learning online visibility the hard way — the wins and the failures both. I've had real reviews vanish overnight. I've watched good businesses stay invisible while the loud ones got all the calls. I learned what actually works, and I got tired of watching hardworking local people miss out on it.

Why the directory exists

Getting found online in 2026 isn't just about Google anymore. More and more people ask AI tools like ChatGPT to recommend a local business — and most small businesses have no idea whether they even show up. The businesses that get named win the job. Everyone else is invisible.

I built this directory so local businesses in our community could have a real, professional page — built the right way, so both Google and AI search can find and recommend them. For free. Because a rising tide lifts all ships, and a community that's easy to find is stronger than any one of us alone.

My promise to you
  • Your directory page is free. No cost, no ads, no obligation, ever.
  • You approve your page before it goes live. It's yours.
  • I'm a real local person with a name and a track record — not a robocall or a faceless company.
  • If I ever offer paid help, it's only because you asked, for real work I actually do.

On being "just another message"

I know your inbox and phone are full of scams, robocalls, and pushy solicitations. I get it — I get them too. So I'm not going to chase you or pressure you. I'd rather earn your trust by showing up consistently, giving first, and letting the results speak. If you want in, the door's open. If not, no hard feelings — I'm still glad you're part of the community.

Common questions

Is this trustworthy, or is it a scam?

It's a genuine free service. It's run by a local business owner with a real name, real local businesses, and a public track record. Listings are free with no ads and no obligation, and you approve your page before it goes live.

Why is it free? What's the catch?

There's no catch on the listing. Free professional pages help the whole community get found, which is the whole point. Optional paid help (like gathering reviews) exists only if you ask for it. The free page never requires payment.

Who does it serve?

Local businesses across Southwest Florida — Venice, Nokomis, Osprey, Sarasota, North Port, Port Charlotte, and Englewood — of all kinds, from trades and home services to restaurants and shops.
