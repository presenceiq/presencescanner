About Top Priority Lawns & More

Top Priority Lawns & More is a family owned lawn care and landscaping company covering Port Charlotte, North Port, Englewood, Punta Gorda and Venice, Florida. Ten years in the trade.

The regular work is mowing, edging, weed control, cleanups, trimming, and small tree and shrub maintenance. Sidewalks and driveways get blown off before they leave, so nothing is left sitting on the concrete. In the off season they also take on gutter cleaning and pressure washing, which means the same people can look after a property the whole year rather than only through the growing season.

One thing not to do

On your Google Business Profile there's a box for your website. Don't put your PresenceScanner page there. That box is for your own website. This page is part of the PresenceScanner directory, not your own website, and Google has a rule against pointing that box at somebody else's page.

If you don't have a website, leave that box empty. Anywhere else is fine though. Your own website, your Facebook page, your emails, your business cards.

Google's rule, if you want to read it: support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
