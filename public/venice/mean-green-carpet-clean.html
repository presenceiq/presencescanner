What sets Mean Green apart

In the owner's words: "We specialize in non-toxic, eco-friendly, soap-free cleaning methods. We proudly use Green Seal certified products which are safe for your family and your pets. We utilize the deepest and most effective method available, steam cleaning or hot water extraction. No chemical residue, dirt or allergens left behind. We offer up-front pricing that doesn't change when we get to your home."

The soap-free part matters more than it sounds. Most carpet cleaning leaves detergent behind in the fibres, and that residue attracts dirt, which is why a carpet can look dirty again within weeks of being cleaned. Hot water extraction without soap leaves nothing in the carpet once it dries.

Frequently asked questions

What does soap-free cleaning actually mean?

Most carpet cleaning leaves detergent behind in the fibres, and that residue attracts dirt so the carpet gets dirty again faster. Mean Green use hot water extraction without soap, so nothing is left in the carpet once it dries.

Is it safe for pets and children?

The products are Green Seal certified, which is an independent standard for safer cleaning products. No chemical residue and no harsh odours afterwards.

What is steam cleaning, or hot water extraction?

Hot water is forced into the carpet or tile under pressure and immediately extracted along with the dirt. It reaches deeper than surface cleaning and it is the method most carpet manufacturers recommend.

Will the price change when they arrive?

No. They quote up front and that is what you pay. In their words, pricing that does not change when we get to your home.
