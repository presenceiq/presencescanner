About Rainbow Financial

Rainbow Financial Consulting works with small service businesses that are struggling with cash flow. The usual client is a marketing agency, an IT firm or a law practice with money sitting in unpaid invoices. The work covers the whole order-to-cash cycle: billing, financial reports and analysis, chasing outstanding invoices, and consultations on chargebacks and debt collection. Accounting services, but not tax preparation.

What sets Rainbow Financial apart

In the owner's words: "We follow the whole order to cash cycle. If clients need billing help all the way to calling the clients for payment or help filing for debt collections. We actually care about helping businesses succeed and keep more profits. They might need a solution that we don't offer, and we are honest and will tell them. We will help interview for an in house role or find the best solution for them."

That last part is the unusual bit. A consultant who will tell you that you need an employee rather than a consultant, and then help you hire one, is not selling you the thing they happen to have.

And the specialism is narrower than it sounds. Plenty of people will do your books. Far fewer will pick up the phone and chase an invoice that is ninety days out.

Frequently asked questions

What is the order-to-cash cycle?

Everything between doing the work and having the money in your account. Raising the invoice, sending it, tracking it, chasing it, and escalating it if it does not get paid. Most small businesses handle the first part well and the rest badly.

Do they do taxes?

No. Accounting services, financial reports and analysis, but not tax preparation.

What kind of businesses is this for?

Service businesses, typically marketing agencies, IT firms and law practices. The common thread is invoicing clients rather than taking payment at the point of sale.

Will they actually chase my customers for payment?

Yes. That is part of the service, through to helping file for debt collection if it comes to that.

What if I need something they don't offer?

They will tell you. In the owner's words, they will help interview for an in-house role or find the best solution, even when that is not them.

What areas do they cover?

North Port, Sarasota County and Charlotte County, and remotely beyond that.
