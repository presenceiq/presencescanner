About Gutter Crafters

Gutter Crafters LLC installs, replaces, repairs and cleans seamless gutters for homes across North Port, Port Charlotte, Venice, Englewood, Punta Gorda and Sarasota, Florida. Alongside the gutters themselves, the work covers downspouts, gutter guards and leaf protection, soffit and fascia, and screen repair. The point of all of it is the same: keeping rainwater away from the walls and the foundation, where drainage problems and water damage begin.

Frequently asked questions

What areas does Gutter Crafters serve?

North Port, Port Charlotte, Venice, Englewood, Punta Gorda and Sarasota, Florida.

What are seamless gutters and why do they matter?

Seamless gutters are formed in one continuous length to fit the run of your roof, so there are no joints along the middle. Joints are where sectional gutters usually start leaking, so removing them removes the most common failure point.

Do gutters really matter in Florida?

They matter here more than most places. Heavy summer downpours dump a great deal of water in a short time, and without gutters that water runs straight down the wall and pools at the foundation, which is where drainage problems and water damage start.

Do they fix soffit and fascia as well as gutters?

Yes. Soffit and fascia installation and repair are part of the service, which matters because failing gutters often damage the fascia behind them. Screen repair is also offered.

Are gutter guards worth fitting?

They cut down how often gutters need clearing, which is worth considering if the house sits under trees or the gutters are awkward to reach. Gutter guards and leaf protection are installed as part of the service.

Who will I be dealing with?

The owner, Jacob Blomgren. Customers work directly with him rather than a salesperson, and he stands behind the work himself.
