About Buyers Choice Inspections

Buyers Choice Inspections is Henry Finck's company. Full home inspections, 4-point inspections, wind mitigations and mold testing, across Venice, Nokomis, Osprey, North Port, Englewood, Port Charlotte, Sarasota and Punta Gorda.

The number worth knowing is the turnaround. He works Monday to Sunday and sends the report to the client and the agent the same day. Both reviews on this page mention getting the report back quickly, and one of those customers needed it for an insurance company.

What sets Buyers Choice Inspections apart

In the owner's words: "After 10 years in the construction industry, we have a wide variety of knowledge we bring to this field. We work Monday- Sunday and we send all reports to the client/agent same day to respect quick turn around times."

Three different numbers describe his experience and none of them contradict the others. 10 years in construction before he started inspecting, 7 years inspecting, and 2 and a half years running Buyers Choice. One thing not to do

On your Google Business Profile there's a box for your website. Don't put your PresenceScanner page there. That box is for your own website. This page is part of the PresenceScanner directory, not your own website, and Google has a rule against pointing that box at somebody else's page.

If you don't have a website, leave that box empty. Anywhere else is fine though. Your own website, your Facebook page, your emails, your business cards.

Google's rule, if you want to read it: support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
