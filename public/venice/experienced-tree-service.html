Experienced Tree Service LLC

About Experienced Tree Service LLC

Experienced Tree Service LLC is Joseph Hatina's company, in business since 2008 with 20 years in the trade behind it. Tree removals, trimming and stump grinding, from Venice and Sarasota down to Punta Gorda, and north into St. Petersburg, Pinellas and Hillsborough.

The thing worth knowing about them is the crane. Most tree companies climb and rope sections down. A crane takes the weight off the tree before it is cut, which is what you want when the tree is over a roof, a pool cage or a driveway, and it is also how big removals get done in one day instead of three.

What they do

Tree removal
Taking whole trees down, including the ones in tight spots next to a house.
Crane assisted removal
In their own words, crane assisted removals. The crane carries each section out rather than it being roped down through the branches.
Tree trimming
Trimming and shaping rather than removal.
Large stump grinding
They call out large stump grinding specifically, which not every outfit is set up for.
Emergency tree service
Storm work. One of the reviews below is a hurricane job where two foremen came out to assess the damage and the crew started the next day.

What sets Experienced Tree Service LLC apart

One thing not to do

On your Google Business Profile there's a box for your website. Don't put your PresenceScanner page there. That box is for your own website. This page is part of the PresenceScanner directory, not your own website, and Google has a rule against pointing that box at somebody else's page.

If you don't have a website, leave that box empty. Anywhere else is fine though. Your own website, your Facebook page, your emails, your business cards.

Google's rule, if you want to read it: support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
