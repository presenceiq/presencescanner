About CMPCleaning Venice

CMP Cleaning is a small, family-owned cleaning service that's kept homes and vacation rentals spotless across Venice, Nokomis, Osprey, and the surrounding area for over 18 years. Vacation rental cleaning and fast VRBO and Airbnb turnovers are our specialty, but we also handle regular house cleaning, deep cleans, and move-in/move-out cleaning. Because we're owner-operated, you get the same trusted, detail-focused work every visit, no rotating crews, no cut corners. Local, reliable, and glad to earn your trust.
