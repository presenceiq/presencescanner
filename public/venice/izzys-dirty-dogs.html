About Izzy’s Dirty Dogs

Izzy’s Dirty Dogs is a mobile dog grooming service run by Alissa Magrath, covering North Port, Port Charlotte, Venice and Nokomis. The grooming is done at your home, so the dog does not ride in a car and does not wait in a kennel for its turn. Alissa has eleven years grooming dogs, and Izzy’s Dirty Dogs is her own business, newly opened.

What sets Izzy’s Dirty Dogs apart

In the owner’s words, what she is after is "creating a positive grooming experience where every dog is treated with patience, compassion, and care. it’s all about building trust, forming relationships with pets and their families, and making every dog feel safe, loved, and looking their absolute best."

The practical version of that is the van. A dog that hates the car never gets in one, and a nervous dog is groomed in its own driveway instead of a room full of other dogs. Aaron’s review above is exactly that, and it is the reason people book a mobile groomer rather than the cheapest one.

Frequently asked questions

Do you come to the house?

One thing not to do

On your Google Business Profile there’s a box for your website. Don’t put your PresenceScanner page there. That box is for your own website. This page is part of the PresenceScanner directory, not your own website, and Google has a rule against pointing that box at somebody else’s page.

If you don’t have a website, leave that box empty. Anywhere else is fine though. Your own website, your Facebook page, your emails, your business cards.

Google’s rule, if you want to read it: support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
