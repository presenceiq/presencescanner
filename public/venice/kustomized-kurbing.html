About Kustomized Kurbing

Kustomized Kurbing installs decorative concrete curbing, stone, paver walkways, patios and retaining walls across Punta Gorda, Port Charlotte, Englewood, North Port, Nokomis, Venice, Osprey, Laurel and Palmer Ranch. They also do full landscape installations including palms and sod. They do not mow, trim trees or handle irrigation, which is deliberate: they do the hard landscaping and they do it properly.

What sets Kustomized Kurbing apart

In the owner's words: "We pride ourselves on practicality and honesty. A lot of times I will go to a client's house and spend a great majority of the time talking them out of doing certain things."

That is unusual and it is worth taking seriously. Curbing around an oak tree will be lifted by the roots within a year or two. Curbing along a driveway gets driven over, which happens every time. Being told that before you pay for it is worth more than being sold it.

They also stand behind the work beyond the warranty. There is a one-year warranty, but when a batch of concrete turned out badly they ripped out and replaced 325 feet in Gran Paradiso three years after the job, at no charge to the customer.

Frequently asked questions

What is decorative concrete curbing?

A continuous concrete border poured in place, usually separating a stone or mulch bed from grass. It is stamped and coloured, and because it is poured as one piece there are no joints for weeds to come through.

Where should curbing not go?

Around an oak tree, because the roots will lift it, usually within a year or two. And along a driveway, because it gets driven over. They will tell you this before you pay for it rather than after.

Do they do the planting as well as the hardscaping?

Yes. Full landscape installations including palms and sod, alongside the curbing, stone and paver work.

What do they not do?

Mowing, tree trimming and irrigation. That is deliberate, so the work they do take on is the work they specialise in.

What areas do they cover?

Punta Gorda, Port Charlotte, Englewood, North Port, Nokomis, Venice, Osprey, Laurel and Palmer Ranch in South Sarasota.

How long is the warranty?

One year. In practice they have gone well beyond it, replacing 325 feet of curbing in Gran Paradiso three years after the job when a batch of concrete turned out badly.
