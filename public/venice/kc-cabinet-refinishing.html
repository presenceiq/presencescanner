About K&C Cabinet Refinishing

K&C Cabinet Refinishing & Painting is a family-run business that gives tired kitchen and bathroom cabinets a brand-new look without the cost of replacing them. They refinish cabinets in any color you choose, and it doesn't matter whether the doors are wood, laminate, or thermafoil — the finished result looks new. Serving North Port, Venice, Sarasota, and communities across Southwest Florida, K&C is built on quality over quantity: the owner, his son, and his wife are the only ones you'll see on the job.
