About Legacy Paintworks

Legacy Paintworks LLC is a painting contractor serving Charlotte County and the surrounding Southwest Florida area, backed by more than 10 years of hands-on experience in the field. As owner Joshua Betancourt puts it: "We don't just paint homes — we build lasting relationships through honest communication, meticulous craftsmanship, and treating every home like it's our own. At Legacy Paintworks, we're building a legacy of quality, one brushstroke at a time." From full interior and exterior repaints to cabinets, drywall, custom finishes, and pressure washing, the work is done with care and a free estimate up front.
