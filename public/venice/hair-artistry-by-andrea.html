About Hair Artistry by Andrea

Hair Artistry by Andrea is an independent private studio in Port Charlotte, run by Andrea McVicker. Color is the specialism: dimensional color, lived-in balayage, blonding, highlights, gloss and toner, and advanced color correction, alongside precision cuts, blowouts, Keratin Complex Express smoothing and Malibu C treatments. Twenty-four years in, and a nationally award-winning colorist. Clients travel in from Punta Gorda, Deep Creek, North Port, Englewood, Rotonda, Venice and Nokomis.

What sets Hair Artistry apart

In the owner's words: "I am a nationally award-winning hair colorist with 25+ years of experience, but what truly sets me apart is my unyielding commitment to hair integrity over profit. I will turn down a chemical service before I compromise the structural health of your hair, and I never use heat lamps to speed-process color or bleach. My studio operates as a quiet, true one-on-one environment: I never double-book, so your appointment is 100% focused on you."

Three specific things there, and each one costs her money. Turning down a chemical service is turning down the booking. Not using heat lamps makes every color appointment longer. Not double-booking means fewer clients in a day.

Lori's review above is the same point from the other side: concerned about the integrity of her hair, which she loved, as not all stylists are.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the studio?

Port Charlotte. It is an independent private studio rather than a salon floor, and clients travel in from Punta Gorda, Deep Creek, Lake Suzy, North Port, Englewood, Rotonda, Venice and Nokomis.

What does one client at a time actually mean?

No double-booking. Your appointment is not shared with someone else's color processing, so the whole session is yours. Most salons run two or three chairs at once.

Why does she refuse some services?

Because some hair will not survive the chemical process being asked of it. In her own words, she will turn down a chemical service before compromising the structural health of the hair. That is an unusual thing for a colorist to say out loud and it is the reason to book her.

What is wrong with heat lamps?

They speed up color and bleach processing, which is convenient for the salon and harder on the hair. She does not use them. It makes appointments longer and the result more predictable.

What is she known for specifically?

Color. Dimensional color, lived-in balayage, blonding, full and partial highlights, root touch-ups, gloss and toner, and advanced color correction. Cuts, blowouts and smoothing treatments alongside it.

Does she do bridal and event hair?

Yes, weddings and homecoming, plus facial waxing.

Can I book online?

Yes, through andreahairartistry.glossgenius.com, or call the studio directly.

How long has she been doing this?

24 years, and she is a nationally award-winning colorist.
