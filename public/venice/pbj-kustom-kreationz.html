About PB&J Kustom Kreationz

PB&J Kustom Kreationz is a handmade gift shop in North Port. Jennifer makes custom shirts and hoodies, personalized tumblers, laser engraved cutting boards and ornaments, embroidery for businesses, vinyl decals, wax melts, candles, and cold process soaps and body care. Everything is made to order in her home studio.

It covers a wider spread than most gift makers, because the equipment overlaps. The laser that engraves a whiskey decanter set also does the metal bookmarks and the Christmas ornaments. The embroidery machine doing a memorial piece for one customer does a run of staff polos for the shop down the road the next week. Orders go out locally and ship nationwide through Etsy, Amazon and TikTok.

What sets PB&J Kustom Kreationz apart

In the owner's words: "Everything is made by one pair of hands, mine, in small batches in North Port, never mass-produced or drop-shipped. I personalize almost anything (names, photos, logos, memorial pieces), my cold process soaps are made coconut-free, and businesses can order embroidered apparel with no contract and no minimum. Spreading joy, one Kustom Kreation at a time."

Two of those are checkable claims rather than slogans. Coconut free cold process soap matters to anyone with the allergy, and most handmade soap uses coconut oil. No contract and no minimum matters to a small business that wants six polos rather than sixty.

Frequently asked questions

What does PB&J Kustom Kreationz make?

Custom shirts, sweatshirts and hoodies, personalized tumblers, laser engraved cutting boards and ornaments, embroidery for businesses and events, vinyl car decals, metal bookmarks, wax melts and soy candles, and handmade soaps, lotions and lip care.

Can you put my business logo on shirts or polos for my staff?

Yes. Embroidered polos and tees are one of the services, logo digitizing is included, and there is no contract and no minimum order.

Do you ship, or is this local pickup only?

Both. Everything is made in North Port, and orders ship nationwide through Etsy, Amazon and TikTok shops.

Can you personalize a gift with a photo or someone's name?

Yes. In the owner's words, almost anything can be personalized, including names, photos, logos and memorial pieces.

Are the cold process soaps coconut free?

Yes. The cold process soaps are made coconut free. The melt and pour bars are a limited run and are not coconut free.

What is a subscription club?

A monthly package sent out on a subscription. There are three to choose from: wax melts and candles, tumblers, and shirts.

Who actually makes the products?

Jennifer makes them herself, in small batches in a home studio in North Port. In her own words, nothing is mass produced or drop shipped.

One thing not to do

On your Google Business Profile there's a box for your website. Don't put your PresenceScanner page there. That box is for your own website. This page is part of the PresenceScanner directory, not your own website, and Google has a rule against pointing that box at somebody else's page.

If you don't have a website, leave that box empty. Anywhere else is fine though. Your own website, your Facebook page, your emails, your business cards.
