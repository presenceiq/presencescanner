About Plantation Shutters

Plantation Shutters supplies and installs interior plantation shutters, wood blinds and roller shades for homes in Venice, Sarasota and Bradenton, Florida, covering Sarasota and Manatee counties. Owner Hector Aguilar has spent 36 years in window treatments and runs the business himself, so the person who measures your windows is the person who fits them. The work is interior only, with the focus on getting the fit and the light right rather than selling a catalogue.

What sets Plantation Shutters apart

In the owner's words: "The years of experience at the service of my clients make me recommend future work is my best motivation to continue doing a good job."

In practice that means 36 years of measuring and fitting, an owner who does the work rather than sending a crew, and a business that has run on repeat customers and referrals rather than advertising.

Frequently asked questions

What areas does Plantation Shutters serve?

Venice, Sarasota and Bradenton, Florida, covering Sarasota and Manatee counties.

What window treatments do they supply?

Interior plantation shutters, wood blinds and roller shades. The focus is interior windows for homes rather than exterior or storm products.

Who does the measuring and the installation?

Owner Hector Aguilar. It is a small owner-run business, so the person who measures your windows is the person who fits them.

How much experience does the owner have?

36 years in window treatments. In his own words, that experience at the service of his clients, and the work they recommend afterwards, is what keeps him doing a good job.

Do plantation shutters suit Florida homes?

They are a common choice here because the louvers let you control glare and privacy without blocking the window, and they hold up better than fabric treatments in strong sun. Material choice matters in humidity, which is worth discussing before ordering.
