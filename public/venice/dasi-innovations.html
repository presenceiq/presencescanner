About DASI Innovations

DASI Innovations builds and installs custom closets, pantries, wall units and laundry cabinets, and singles out garage storage as a specialty. Jessica Scheid runs it, and the business has 15 years behind it.

The thing worth knowing before you call is that consultations cost nothing. For work that gets built to fit a specific room, that matters, because the measuring and the design are the part you cannot judge from a photo.

What they build

Custom closets
Closets built and installed to fit the space rather than assembled from stock sizes.
Pantries
Kitchen pantry shelving and storage built into the room.
Wall units
Built-in wall units made for the wall they go on.
Laundry cabinets
Cabinets and shelving fitted around the washer and dryer.
Garage storage
Garage storage systems. This is the one they call out as a specialty rather than list alongside everything else.
Consultations
Free. Their word for it is complimentary.

What sets DASI Innovations apart

In the owner's words: "We offer a personalized experience, quality craftsmanship, and a solution designed around your home and lifestyle. We customize every space to make it perfect for you."

The part you can check for yourself is further down this page. Read the two reviews and count the names. Between them, customers name six different people across getting quoted, scheduling, measuring, designing and installing. That is not one person with a van. It is a team big enough that the design and the install are done by different people, which is why a free consultation is worth something here.

One thing not to do

On your Google Business Profile there's a box for your website. Don't put your PresenceScanner page there. That box is for your own website. This page is part of the PresenceScanner directory, not your own website, and Google has a rule against pointing that box at somebody else's page.

If you don't have a website, leave that box empty. Anywhere else is fine though. Your own website, your Facebook page, your emails, your business cards.

Google's rule, if you want to read it: support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
