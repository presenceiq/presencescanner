About VoltWave Electric

VoltWave Electric is a licensed Florida electrical contractor, license EC13015428. Evelio has 12 years in the trade and has been running this business for 2 of them.

The unusual part is the spread. Most electricians stop at the panel. This one carries on into the things that get wired at the same time, so the cameras, the Wi-Fi, the automation and the audio are the same company on the same visit rather than three more numbers to call.

In the owner's words: "VoltWave Electric combines licensed electrical work with smart home technology, security, networking, audio, and generator solutions under one company. One thing not to do

On your Google Business Profile there's a box for your website. Don't put your PresenceScanner page there. That box is for your own website. This page is part of the PresenceScanner directory, not your own website, and Google has a rule against pointing that box at somebody else's page.

If you don't have a website, leave that box empty. Anywhere else is fine though. Your own website, your Facebook page, your emails, your business cards.

Google's rule, if you want to read it: support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
