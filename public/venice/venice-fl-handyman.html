About Venice FL Handyman

Venice FL Handyman handles home repairs large and small, done in a timely, workmanlike manner. The focus is careful prep, clean work areas, and clear communication so you always know what's happening with your project. Whether you need one repair or a full punch-list of fixes, he'll walk the job with you, give you a straightforward price, and get it done. With 45 years in the home repair business, he serves Venice, Nokomis, Osprey, Sarasota, Englewood, North Port, Port Charlotte, and the surrounding areas.
