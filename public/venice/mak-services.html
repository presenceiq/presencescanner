About MAK Services

MAK Services is Michael A. Keller, working out of Rotonda West with 40 years behind him. In his own words it is “an electric equipment service and repair business.” That covers more ground than it sounds like it does.

On the computer side: laptop repairs, hard drive problems, blue screens, WiFi and printer connection trouble, Microsoft account troubleshooting, and sitting down with somebody to work out their iPhone apps and their storage. On the other side his card lists instrumentation and controls, and that is why both of the reviews below are about a pool salt chlorinator rather than a computer. Same skill, different box.

One thing not to do

On your Google Business Profile there's a box for your website. Don't put your PresenceScanner page there. That box is for your own website. This page is part of the PresenceScanner directory, not your own website, and Google has a rule against pointing that box at somebody else's page.

If you don't have a website, leave that box empty. Anywhere else is fine though. Your own website, your Facebook page, your emails, your business cards.

Google's rule, if you want to read it: support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
