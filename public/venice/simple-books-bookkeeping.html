Common questions

What does Simple Books Bookkeeping do?

QuickBooks-certified bookkeeping for small businesses — ongoing books management, payroll support, and QuickBooks account setup and maintenance. The focus is keeping your finances accurate, organized, and current so you can spend less time on paperwork and more time running your business.

What areas do they serve?

Small businesses across Southwest Florida, including Sarasota, Venice, North Port, Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda, and Tampa. Because the work is handled remotely and digitally, businesses throughout these areas can work with Simple Books without meeting in person.

Are they QuickBooks certified?

Yes. Simple Books Bookkeeping is QuickBooks certified and handles QuickBooks setup, ongoing maintenance, and payroll support — so your books are set up correctly from the start and kept accurate over time, making tax season and financial decisions far less stressful.
