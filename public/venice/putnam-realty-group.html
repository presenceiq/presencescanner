About Putnam Realty Group

Putnam Realty Group specializes in home selling across Sarasota and Charlotte County — including full-service representation and flat-fee MLS listing options — for sellers in Venice, Nokomis, Osprey, Casey Key, Wellen Park, North Port, Port Charlotte, and Englewood. Whether you're a first-time seller, relocating from out of state, or exploring how to sell with or without a traditional agent, Putnam Realty Group offers flexible solutions tailored to your goals.

Real-world experience

Michael Putnam's real estate experience is hands-on and extensive. He has personally owned and managed 27 properties — ranging from single-family homes and condos to beachfront residences, mobile homes, long-term rentals, and vacation rental properties — in markets from New England to Florida and internationally. That background means guidance grounded in real-world insight, not just theory. He works with homeowners who want to understand all of their selling options, from traditional full-service representation to flexible service models and lower-fee MLS listing solutions, helping each seller choose the approach that fits their situation, comfort level, and timeline.
