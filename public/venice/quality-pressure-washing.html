About Quality Pressure Washing

Quality Pressure Washing is an exterior cleaning company serving Port Charlotte, Rotonda West, Englewood, Venice, North Port, Punta Gorda, North Fort Myers, Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Estero, Bonita Springs and Naples, Florida. Owner Tim Cook has been in the trade for over 25 years.

The work covers siding, pool cages, pool decks, lanais, patios, driveways, walkways and fences. Quality Pressure Washing also seals brick pavers, which is the step that protects the surface and holds the color once the cleaning is done. The company is licensed and insured, and references are available on request.

Satisfaction guaranteed or it's on us. That's how Tim Cook puts it, and after 25 years in the trade he has the references to back it up. The company is licensed and insured, and he'll supply references to anyone who asks before booking.

One thing not to do

On your Google Business Profile there's a box for your website. Don't put your PresenceScanner page there. That box is for your own website. This page is part of the PresenceScanner directory, not your own website, and Google has a rule against pointing that box at somebody else's page.

If you don't have a website, leave that box empty. Anywhere else is fine though. Your own website, your Facebook page, your emails, your business cards.

Google's rule, if you want to read it: support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
