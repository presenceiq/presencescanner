About EM Garage Door Service

EM Garage Door Service, Inc. is a mobile garage door company covering Cape Coral and Southwest Florida. Residential and commercial, repair, installation and maintenance, with nine years in the trade behind it.

Mobile means what it says. They come to the house, and there is no Cape Coral storefront to drive to. In their own words on the listing form: “We travel to customers; no walk-in Cape Coral storefront. Call to confirm availability at your address.”

That matters more with a garage door than with most trades, because a door that has come off its track or dropped a spring is not going anywhere. The van has to come to you.

Owner Eran Mitler says the company runs several technicians in Cape Coral around the clock, with immediate dispatch, same-day repairs and 24/7 emergency service, and that they can be at a Cape Coral property within the hour. That arrival time is his, and it is for Cape Coral. He asked that it not be read across to the other areas on this page.

If your door has just come off its track, read this first

This is EM Garage Door Service’s own guidance, written out by the owner for this page. It is here because a door off its track is the thing people search for in a panic, usually with the car still inside.

Stop operating the door. Do not press the remote or the wall button again, do not force it open or closed, and do not try to put the rollers back into the track.

Do not pull the emergency release cord on a door that is off-track, tilted or unstable. Do not loosen brackets or adjust springs, cables or drums. The lifting components may still be under tension, and the door can move or fall without warning.

Keep people, pets and vehicles away. Do not walk or drive underneath a door that is partly open or hanging crooked. Use another entrance while you wait.

Then call a garage door professional and leave the door where it is. If it is safe to do it from a distance, take a photo, and say what you can see. Whether a cable is loose, whether the door is hanging unevenly, whether the opening can be secured at all. That tells the technician what is coming before he arrives.

If anyone is trapped or injured, call emergency services first.

When they get there, the technician inspects the tracks, rollers, cables, springs and opener, explains what he found along with the estimate, and checks the door runs properly once the approved repair is done.
