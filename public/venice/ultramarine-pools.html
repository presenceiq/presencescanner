About Ultramarine Pools

Ultramarine Pools is a pool service and repair company covering Punta Gorda, Port Charlotte, North Port, Venice, Sarasota and much of Southwest Florida. The company was established in 2008, owner Darren Grovatt has been servicing pools since 2003, and the lead mechanic has been in the industry since 1979. Technicians are State Certified, and routes are deliberately capped at roughly 15 pools a day rather than the 20 to 30 that is common in the trade.

What sets Ultramarine Pools apart

In the owner's words: "Unlike most Pool Service companies, who overload technicians with 20 to 30 or more pools a day, we limit the amount of stops to roughly 15 a day, which prevents skimping or skipping on Chemicals or maintenance items, for an overall thorough visit and quality.

Most Pool Services perform the physical maintenance as needed, but Ultramarine Pools is more thorough. We come prepared to visit each Pool in one trip, instead of multiple visits back and forth to the truck, which could lead to skimping or skipping on maintenance items or chemicals. Performing all the necessary physical maintenance like: Skimming, Brushing, Soaping the Tile, Spraying the Tile & Coping for Mold, & Emptying Baskets. We also provide After Photos of your Pool, time-stamped pictures Vacuuming with portable Hammerhead Vacuums, which cut down on wear and tear on your Pools Equipment, Pump and Filter, and also photos any time your Filter is Cleaned.

Also, our lead mechanic has been in the industry since 1979! Our technicians are State Certified, holding a Certificate that only about 20% of technicians in the field have, which allows us to keep the same face in your backyard. We respect your time and privacy by arriving on the same day each week. We answer the phone and keep up the highest level communication. We include all of the physical maintenance and chemicals, as well as Phosphate Treatments."

Frequently asked questions

How many pools does a technician visit in a day?

Roughly 15. Many pool service companies run 20 to 30 or more stops a day. Ultramarine caps the route deliberately so that chemicals and maintenance items do not get skimped or skipped on the last few pools of the day.

Is Ultramarine Pools licensed in Florida?

Yes. Ultramarine Pools, LLC holds Florida Certified Pool/Spa Contractor License CPC1459427, current and active through August 2028. Anyone can confirm it at myfloridalicense.com.

What is included in a regular service visit?

Skimming, brushing, soaping the tile, spraying tile and coping for mold, emptying baskets, all chemicals, and phosphate treatments. Vacuuming is done with portable Hammerhead vacuums rather than running the pool's own pump and filter.

How do I know the work was actually done?

Time-stamped after photos of the pool are provided on each visit, plus photos any time the filter is cleaned.

Will the same person service my pool each week?

That is the aim. Technicians are State Certified, a certificate only around 20 percent of technicians in the field hold, which helps keep the same face in your backyard. Visits are on the same day each week.

What areas does Ultramarine Pools serve?

Punta Gorda, Babcock Ranch, Burnt Store, Cape Coral, North Fort Myers, North Port, Harbor Heights, Lake Suzy, Deep Creek, Whiskey Creek, Gulf Cove, Rotonda West, Placida, Port Charlotte, Englewood, West Port, Wellen Park, Venice, Nokomis, Laurel, Osprey, Siesta Key, Casey Key, and Sarasota.
