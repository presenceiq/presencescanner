Frequently asked questions

Do they replace windows, or only repair them?

Repair only, and that is deliberate. If a window balance has failed or a slider will not move, they fix it rather than sell you a new one. In their own words, they are not salesmen, they are technicians.

What can actually be repaired rather than replaced?

Sliding doors, pocket doors, interior and exterior doors, window balances including coil balances and crank windows, glass, handles and locks.

How quickly can they come out?

Same-day repair is offered, and estimates are free.

What is a window balance and why does it fail?

It is the mechanism inside the frame that holds a window open. When it fails the window drops shut on its own or will not stay up. It is one of the most common window faults and one of the most commonly replaced unnecessarily.
