About Honey Do Painting and Handyman Services

Honey Do Painting and Handyman Services LLC covers painting and general home repair across Sarasota and Charlotte County, Florida. Owner Dillon Cameron runs it himself, so the person who quotes the job is the person who turns up to do it. The work spans interior and exterior painting, drywall repair, masonry, framing, pressure washing, cleaning and landscaping, which suits a house that needs several things sorted at once rather than one trade at a time.

What sets Honey Do apart

In the owner's words: "100% focused on customer satisfaction. Attention to detail and reliable communication. We show up on time, respect the home, and get it right the first time. Honest pricing, dependable, top quality."

The detail worth noticing in the review above is the order of the work: the drywall was repaired before the painting started. That is the step that decides whether a paint job still looks right in two years, and it is the one most often skipped.
