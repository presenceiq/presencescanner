Frequently asked questions

Why use a mobile home specialist rather than a general contractor?

Because a manufactured home is built differently. The subfloor, the roof structure and the way a lanai attaches and what it can carry are not the same as a block home. A contractor who works mostly on site-built houses is learning on your property.

Do they do lanais and screen rooms?

Yes, and it is a large part of the work. Lanai builds and remodels, screen rooms, acrylic windows, insulated doors and walls.

What is an aluminum roofover?

A new aluminum roof built over the existing one rather than tearing the old one off. It is common on manufactured homes and it deals with leaks and heat at the same time.

Can they repair a soft or failing subfloor?

Yes. Subfloor repair is one of the listed services and it is one of the more common problems in an older manufactured home.

What areas do they cover?

Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda and Arcadia locally, extending south to North Fort Myers, Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Bonita Springs, Estero and Naples.

How long have they been doing this?

Six years, family owned and operated.
