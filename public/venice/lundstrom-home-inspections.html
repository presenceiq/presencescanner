About Lundstrom Home Inspections

Lundstrom Home Inspections is a home inspection company serving North Port, Venice, Sarasota, Port Charlotte, and Englewood, Florida. Founder Scott Lundstrom spent 15 years working as a handyman in the building trades before becoming a home inspector, so he examines a house with the eye of someone who has actually built and repaired the things he is inspecting. Buyers, sellers, and homeowners come to Lundstrom for clear, honest inspections that explain the real condition of a property in plain language, not jargon.

Inspection services

  • Full home inspection
  • 4-point inspection
  • Wind mitigation inspection
  • Thermal imaging
  • Mold testing
  • Water quality testing
  • Radon testing
  • Consulting

What sets Lundstrom apart

Every inspection includes extras that most companies charge for or skip entirely: a free home maintenance book, free thermal imaging, and a walk-through after the inspection where Scott explains what he found and what it means for you. Reports are detailed and same-day, with repair addendums, supporting photos, and a PDF version you can keep or hand to your agent. The goal is simple: you finish the process understanding your home, not holding a report you cannot read.
