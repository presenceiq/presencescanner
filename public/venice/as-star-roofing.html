About A's Star Roofing

When a Southwest Florida homeowner or business searches for a roofer they can count on, A's Star Roofing is a name worth finding. Owner Ken Siharath has spent 15 years roofing across the region, handling both residential and commercial jobs on every major roof type: shingle, tile, metal, TPO, and flat systems. The work runs from a single leak repair all the way to a full roof replacement, and when a roof fails suddenly, they respond fast on emergencies. The focus is simple and consistent: careful workmanship, straight pricing, and roofs built to hold up to Florida weather for the long haul.
