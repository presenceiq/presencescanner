About Lemon & Lavender Cleaning

Lemon & Lavender Cleaning LLC is a family-owned cleaning company covering Venice, Englewood, Nokomis, North Port, Rotonda and Sarasota, Florida. Serena Sousa and Barbara handle the work themselves rather than sending a rotating crew, which is why customers tend to get the same two people each time. The company brings all of its own supplies, and before leaving a job they walk through the house with the customer so anything missed is sorted while they are still there.

Services

  • General house cleaning
  • Deep cleaning
  • Airbnb & vacation rental turnovers
  • Move-in & move-out cleaning
  • New construction cleaning
  • Office & commercial cleaning
  • Organizing

What sets Lemon & Lavender apart

In the owner's words: "We are a family owned local business, very dedicated to what we do. We strive for excellence and want to make every customer smile. We will provide all of the supplies needed for each cleaning, and before finishing any job we will do a walk through with the customer to ensure they are satisfied with everything."

The walk-through is the part worth noticing. Most cleaners leave and you find out later whether something was missed. Doing it together before they go means it gets fixed on the spot, and it is not something every company offers.
