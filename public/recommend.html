Who to recommend

Someone in a local group asks for a trade. Find it, tap Copy, paste the comment.

Do this first, before you leave Facebook Tap the comment box on their post and type a word. Facebook usually holds your place while you come here and go back. Leave without typing and the post has a habit of vanishing.
Recommending your own business? Say so. Change the first line to something like “That one’s mine, so I’m biased.” Being upfront about it is the difference between a neighbor helping out and an advert.

Put this on your phone

  1. iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the share button (the square with an arrow coming out of it, top right), scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right, then Add to Home screen.
  3. It becomes an icon like any other app. One tap from your home screen instead of hunting through bookmarks.

On a computer

  1. Chrome or Edge: look at the right-hand end of the address bar, where the web address is. An install icon appears there on this page. Click it, then click Install. It opens in its own window and you can pin it to your taskbar.
  2. If you don't see that icon, bookmarking the page works fine. Most people answering a post in a group are on their phone anyway.
