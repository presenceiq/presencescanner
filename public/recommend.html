Who to recommend

Someone in a local group asks for a trade. Find it, tap Copy, paste the comment.

Do this first, before you go fetch the comment to post. Tap the comment box on their post and type a word. It saves your place. Without it you can come back to a feed that has moved on, and finding that post again is harder than it should be.
Posting about your own business? Use the second button. Find your own card and tap “Copy if this is your business”. That gives you one line and your page address to add to a post you were already writing, in your own words. The first button is written for recommending somebody else, so it reads oddly about yourself.

Put this on your phone

  1. iPhone. Open this page in Safari. Tap the share button at the top right, the square with the arrow coming out of it. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android. Open this page in Chrome. Tap the three dots at the top right. Tap Add to Home screen.
