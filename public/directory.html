Common questions

Is a listing really free?

Yes — a professional page, no cost, no ads, no catch. Hand-built for local businesses in our community.

How does my page help me get found?

Every page is built with structured data (schema) and clear, answer-first content that Google and AI tools like ChatGPT can read and cite — so your business is easier to surface when someone searches or asks an AI for a local recommendation.

Where should I share my page?

Anywhere customers look: your Facebook posts and bio, business cards, email signature, texts, and your Google Business Profile. On Facebook, drop the link in the first comment (not the post) for better reach.

Why does sharing help ALL of us?

This is a business networking group working together. Every page links to the others, so when members share, the whole directory gains authority and every business gets easier to find. Your promotion lifts everyone — and theirs lifts you.

If there are several businesses in my trade, how does AI pick one?

Google and AI tools favor the business they know the most about — the one with the most reviews, the most complete information, and the clearest match to what the customer asked. That's why detail wins: the more specific you are about exactly what you do, where, and what makes you different, the better AI can match you to the right customer. Vague "we do everything" pages blur together; specific pages get picked.

What's the single most important thing I can do to get found?

Google reviews. It's the biggest one, by far. Reviews are a top factor in how Google ranks you AND how AI tools decide which business to recommend. What matters most is a steady flow of real, recent reviews from real customers — not a one-time batch. A business with many fresh reviews gets recommended over a similar business with few. Your directory page gives you the professional foundation; steady real reviews are what push you to the top. (Doing it the right way matters too — asking every customer honestly, never gating or incentivizing, or Google can penalize you.)

How do I get listed?

Fill out the short form (about 3 minutes). Every listing is reviewed personally before the page is built.

What is this directory?

A free, hand-built directory of local businesses across Southwest Florida. It's here to help neighbors find trusted local services, and to help good local businesses get found online — on Google and in AI search. There's no cost to be listed, and no ads.
