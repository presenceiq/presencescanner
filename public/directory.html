What gets listed here

Every listing is read and built by hand. Nothing is added automatically, and not everything that applies gets in.

  • The owner submits it themselves and confirms the details are accurate. No business is added from scraped data or a purchased list.
  • Licenses get checked where a trade has one. Where an owner gives a license number, it is looked up against the state's own records before it goes on the page. Two listings here carry a verified number.
  • Reviews are real customer words, quoted as text, with the customer named. Nothing is invented, bought, or generated, and there are no star ratings anywhere on this site.
  • Each page is written from what the owner actually said, not from a template with the name swapped. That is why they read differently from one another.
  • Some are turned down. A listing has already been declined over a question about licensing that could not be answered. A directory that lists anyone is worth nothing to the businesses on it.

What this is not. A listing here is not an endorsement, and it is not a guarantee of anyone's credentials, insurance, or work. Business details come from the owner. Always confirm a contractor's license and insurance before you hire. Florida licenses can be checked free at myfloridalicense.com.

Common questions

Is a listing really free?

Yes — a professional page, no cost, no ads, no catch. Hand-built for local businesses in our community.

How does my page help me get found?

AI tools read the visible words on a page, so yours opens with a direct answer about what you do and where, and puts real questions and answers in plain text where they can be quoted. That is what gets picked up when someone asks Google or ChatGPT for a local recommendation.

Where should I share my page?

Anywhere customers look: your Facebook posts and bio, business cards, email signature, texts, and your Google Business Profile. On Facebook, drop the link in the first comment (not the post) for better reach.

Why does sharing help ALL of us?

Every page on this directory links to the others, so when members share their page, the whole directory gains authority and every business on it gets easier to find. Your promotion lifts everyone — and theirs lifts you.

If there are several businesses in my trade, how does AI pick one?

Google and AI tools favor the business they know the most about — the one with the most reviews, the most complete information, and the clearest match to what the customer asked. That's why detail wins: the more specific you are about exactly what you do, where, and what makes you different, the better AI can match you to the right customer. Vague "we do everything" pages blur together; specific pages get picked.

What's the single most important thing I can do to get found?

Google reviews. It's the biggest one, by far. Reviews are a top factor in how Google ranks you AND how AI tools decide which business to recommend. What matters most is a steady flow of real, recent reviews from real customers — not a one-time batch. A business with many fresh reviews gets recommended over a similar business with few. Your directory page gives you the professional foundation; steady real reviews are what push you to the top. (Doing it the right way matters too — asking every customer honestly, never gating or incentivizing, or Google can penalize you.)

How do I get listed?

Fill out the short form (about 3 minutes). Every listing is reviewed personally before the page is built.
