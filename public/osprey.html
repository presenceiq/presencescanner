The three things most often missing

When I scan local businesses, the same gaps show up again and again. In order of how common they are:

  1. The phone number — missing or wrong. The single most important thing for getting a call.
  2. The website link — absent, or pointing to the wrong place.
  3. A photo — no logo or business image, which makes a listing look abandoned even when the business is thriving.

Each one is quick to fix. But you can't fix what you can't see.

Yes. If you drive to your customers, you set up as a service-area business and list Osprey among the areas you serve. You can show up just as completely as any storefront. Most contractors don't realize this.

Google shows a list of results. AI often summarizes the options into a much shorter list than traditional search. Getting into that short answer depends on how clearly and consistently your business appears across the web, and the check looks at both.
