Osprey is a small town with big-ticket work.

Osprey isn't a big market by headcount. But anyone who works out here knows the truth: from the waterfront homes on Casey Key and around Blackburn Point to upscale communities like The Oaks and Sorrento Shores, there's serious money and serious work for the trades that homeowners trust.

The numbers back it up. The median home in Osprey runs around $750,000, versus about $450,000 in nearby Venice (2025 sales data). Out on Casey Key, waterfront homes routinely sell in the millions, several recently between $3 and $5 million (Redfin). This is the high end of the county.

The catch? These homeowners usually aren't picking a contractor because they happened to see a truck go by. They research online, read reviews, and increasingly ask AI, before they ever call. When they need a plumber, a painter, a remodeler, a pool guy, a landscaper, they pull out their phone and search "who's the best near me in Osprey?"

So the real question is simple: when an Osprey homeowner searches for your trade, do they find you, or the competitor who showed up better?

Why getting found matters more in a town like Osprey

In a small, affluent market, the math is different than in a big city:

  • Fewer jobs, but bigger ones. You don't need a hundred leads. You need to be the one who shows up when the right homeowner searches.
  • These customers research online first. They're not asking a neighbor for a referral as often. They check Google, reviews, and increasingly AI, before they ever call.
  • Trust is everything at this price point. A complete, professional online presence, photos, reviews, real information, is what makes a high-end homeowner comfortable calling you instead of the next name.

Miss on visibility here, and you're not losing a small job. You're losing the kind of work that keeps you busy for weeks.

Here's the part most contractors miss

When Google or AI recommends a business, it has to understand what that business does, and where.

More than 700 million people use ChatGPT every week (OpenAI), and a high-end homeowner is just as likely to ask AI for a recommendation as to scroll Google. Ask yourself:

  • Does the internet clearly say what you do?
  • Does it clearly say that you serve Osprey and the surrounding area?
  • Is your information the same everywhere it appears?

If any of that is fuzzy, AI stays safe and points the homeowner to the business whose answer is clear.

The three things most often missing

When I scan local businesses around Osprey, the same gaps show up again and again, about 30% are missing at least one of these without realizing it. In order of how common they are:

  1. The phone number, missing or wrong. It's the single most important thing for getting a call: roughly 60% of people who find a local business on their phone reach out by tapping the number right there (Google).
  2. The website link, absent, or pointing to the wrong place.
  3. A photo, no logo or business image, which makes a listing look abandoned even when the business is thriving.

Each one is quick to fix. But you can't fix what you can't see, and it adds up: a complete Google listing gets around 7 times the clicks of a bare one (Google).

Frequently asked questions

Does my business show up when Osprey homeowners search or ask AI?

Maybe. Google and AI recommend businesses whose information is clear, complete, and consistent, and that clearly serve the area. If your details are thin, an Osprey homeowner may never see you. The free check shows you where you stand.

I don't have a shop in Osprey. Can I still show up for it?

Yes. If you drive to your customers, you set up as a service-area business and list Osprey among the areas you serve. You can show up just as completely as any storefront. Most contractors don't realize this.

How is showing up in AI different from ranking on Google?

Google shows a list of results. AI often summarizes the options into a much shorter list than traditional search. Getting into that short answer depends on how clearly and consistently your business appears across the web, and the check looks at both.

How long does it take to show up better?

Some fixes work fast, adding a missing phone number or photo can help within weeks. Building strong, lasting visibility takes a few months of steady effort. Once it's built, it keeps working without ongoing ad spend.
