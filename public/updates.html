If you do one thing, do this

There are now step-by-step instructions on your own directory page for adding your listing to your website. It takes about two minutes, and if someone else manages your site you can forward the whole thing to them.

Why it matters: roughly three quarters of what Google and AI tools know about a business comes from pages the business does not own. Your own website saying you are good counts for very little. Somewhere else saying it counts for a lot. That sentence on your site is one of those mentions, and it is free.

Open your page from the directory and scroll to Add your listing to your website. Your business name and link are already filled in.
