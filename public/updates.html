If you do one thing, do this

There are now step-by-step instructions on your own directory page for adding your listing to your website. It takes about two minutes, and if someone else manages your site you can forward the whole thing to them.

Why it matters: roughly three quarters of what Google and AI tools know about a business comes from pages the business does not own. Your own website saying you are good counts for very little. Somewhere else saying it counts for a lot. That sentence on your site is one of those mentions, and it is free.

20 August 2026
  • Worth doing: claim your free business listings. These are the profiles Google, Bing and AI tools pull from when someone asks for a local recommendation. All free, and most local businesses have only claimed one of them.
    • Google Business Profile first, by a distance. If you already have one, make sure the hours, phone and service area are current.
    • Bing Places next. Hardly anyone bothers, and it matters more than it used to because ChatGPT's search runs on Bing.
    • Apple Business Connect, which is what feeds Apple Maps and Siri.
    • Then Facebook, Yelp, and the free profile at the Better Business Bureau.
    One tip: keep your business name, phone number and service area written exactly the same way on every one. Search engines cross-check them, and small differences cost you.
