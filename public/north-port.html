A note on the North Port / Wellen Park / Venice mix-up

If your business serves the Wellen Park and West Villages area, you already know the confusion: some addresses say North Port, some say Venice, and everyone calls the area Wellen Park. Mail, taxes, and city services are split across the North Port and Venice line.

Here's why that matters for getting found: your customers are just as confused as the maps are. Someone looking for your service might search "North Port," "Wellen Park," or "Venice", all for the same neighborhood. If your business only shows up under one of those names, you're invisible to the people searching the others.

The big marketing companies don't sort this out. They pick one label and move on. Getting it right for your specific area is exactly the kind of hyperlocal detail that puts you ahead of them.
