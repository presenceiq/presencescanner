What I actually see out there

I scan real local businesses around Venice, and about 30% of them are missing pieces of that picture without knowing it.

The most common things missing, in order:

  1. The phone number. Roughly 60% of people who find a local business on their phone reach out by tapping the number right there (Google). If yours is missing or wrong, that tap goes to a competitor — and it's the detail most often absent.
  2. The website link. Missing entirely, or pointing to the wrong place.
  3. A photo. No logo, no picture of the business — which quietly makes a listing look abandoned even when it isn't.

None of these are hard to fix. But you can't fix what you can't see — and it matters more than it sounds: a complete Google listing gets around 7 times the clicks of a bare one (Google). Same business, same town. Just a fuller picture.
