Who To Recommend

Someone in a group asks for a trade. Find it, tap Copy, paste the comment.

Rules that keep this working

  • Recommend the person, never the directory. The link just happens to be where their information lives. Never say "my directory."
  • Your own three businesses have two versions. Plain for your own group, where everyone posts their own. The one that says it's yours for anywhere you're answering a stranger.
  • Link goes in a comment on their post, as a reply to whoever asked. You're answering a question, not advertising.
  • Don't link every single time. Sometimes just name the business and let them search. Admins notice patterns.
  • Start with members you'd send your own mother to. If they handle the lead badly, that lands on you.
  • No match? Write the trade down. That's your recruitment list, in priority order, with proof people are asking.
