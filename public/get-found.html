The short answer

If your business isn't showing up when people search Google or ask AI like ChatGPT, it's almost always one of these:

  1. Your Google Business Profile is missing, unverified, or incomplete. This is the number one reason. Google can't show what isn't set up right.
  2. Your information isn't consistent across the web. Different phone numbers, names, or addresses on different sites confuse Google and AI, so they play it safe and skip you.
  3. You don't have enough reviews. Reviews are one of the biggest factors in whether you show up near the top.
  4. AI can't clearly tell what you do and where. ChatGPT and other AI tools only recommend businesses they clearly understand.

The good news: every one of these is fixable.

Google will tell you how to do it yourself. That's the catch.

Search "how do I get my business on Google" and you'll get a list of steps: claim your profile, set your service area, add photos, get reviews, keep your info consistent everywhere, build a website that answers common questions so AI can read it.

That advice is correct. It's also a lot of work.

Setting it all up properly, and keeping it up, takes real time. Time spent learning Google's system, wrestling with verification, chasing down reviews, and fixing inconsistent listings across a dozen sites is time you're not spending on the work that actually pays you.

That's the real question. Not "can I do this myself?" You probably can. It's "is that the best use of your time?"

For a lot of business owners, the honest answer is no. You're better off doing what you do best and letting someone handle this part.

Here's what actually gets you found

Whether you do it yourself or hand it off, this is what moves the needle:

  • A complete, verified Google Business Profile with the right categories, your real service area, hours, photos, and services listed.
  • Consistent information everywhere your business appears, same name, same phone, same address, across every site.
  • A steady flow of reviews from real customers.
  • A simple website that clearly answers common questions, so both Google and AI can understand and recommend you.
  • Being findable in AI search, which pulls from all of the above plus consistent mentions across the web.

None of it is magic. It's just consistent, correct setup, and the patience to let it build.
