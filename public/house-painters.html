What to ask before you hire a painter in Florida

These are the questions that separate a quote you can compare from one you can't. Ask all five of whoever you call, including the ones on this page.

How are you prepping the surface? On Florida stucco the prep is most of the job. Ask what they do about chalking and what gets pressure washed first.

How many coats, and which paint? A quote for two coats of one brand and a quote for one coat of another are not the same quote, and they rarely say so on the paper.

Who is actually doing the work? Ask whether it's the owner, an employee or a sub, and ask who to call if something goes wrong afterward.

Do you carry liability insurance, and can I see it? A painter who carries it will not mind the question.

Is the estimate in writing, with what's included and what isn't? Get the exclusions in writing too. That's where the arguments come from.

Frequently asked questions

How did these painters get on this list?

Each owner asked to be listed and filled in a form about their own business. Their business has to be registered with its county or the state, and a person reads every listing before it goes up. Nobody pays to be here and there is no fee of any kind.

Are they ranked? Is the one at the top the best?

No. They are not ranked, rated or scored, and there are no stars anywhere on this site. The order on this page means nothing. Call whichever ones cover your town.

Are their licenses checked?

No. No license number is looked up here and no credential is verified. What is required is that the business is registered with its county or the state. Checking a license is your job as the customer, and Florida licenses can be checked free at myfloridalicense.com.

Does PresenceScanner take a cut if I hire one of them?

No. There is no commission, no lead fee and no arrangement of any kind. You call them directly on the number shown.

What if my town is not on this page?

What this page is not

A listing here is not an endorsement, and it is not a guarantee of anyone's credentials, insurance or work. Business details come from the owner. Always confirm a contractor's license and insurance before you hire. Florida licenses can be checked free at myfloridalicense.com.
