Google and AI try to understand where your business actually serves customers. Frequently asked questions

Does my Sarasota business show up when people ask ChatGPT or AI?

Maybe. AI tools recommend businesses whose information is clear, complete, and consistent across the web, and that clearly signal the area they serve. If your details are thin or only say "Sarasota" broadly, AI tends to skip you for a competitor with a clearer local footprint. The free check shows you where you stand.

Should I target "Sarasota" or my specific neighborhood?

Both, but don't skip the neighborhood. Showing up for your specific area, Gulf Gate, Siesta Key, Downtown, Palmer Ranch, or wherever you serve, is often what actually wins the nearby customer. "Sarasota" alone is crowded and competitive. Your neighborhood is where you can stand out.

Isn't Google Business Profile only for stores with a physical location?

No. If you drive to your customers, you set up as a "service-area business," list the specific Sarasota areas you serve, and you can show up just as completely as any storefront. Most service businesses don't realize this and miss out.

How is showing up in AI different from ranking on Google?

Google shows a list of results. AI often summarizes the options into a much shorter list than traditional search. Getting into that short answer depends on how clearly and consistently your business appears across Google and the web, and the check looks at both.

How long does it take to show up better?

Some fixes work fast, adding a missing phone number or photo can help within weeks. Building strong, lasting visibility takes a few months of steady effort. Once it's built, it keeps working without ongoing ad spend.
